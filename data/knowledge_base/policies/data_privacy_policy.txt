AEGIS DATA PRIVACY POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy establishes principles for protecting personal data and ensuring that personal data is handled responsibly.

2. Purpose Limitation

Personal data should be collected and processed only for specified, clear, and legitimate purposes.

3. Data Minimization

Only personal data that is necessary for the intended purpose should be collected, accessed, or processed.

4. Authorized Personnel

Access to personal data must be limited to personnel who require the information for legitimate and authorized responsibilities.

5. Data Protection

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

6. Monitoring

Access to personal data may be monitored to identify unauthorized, excessive, or suspicious activity.

7. Suspicious Activity

Unusual access patterns, excessive data access, repeated failed access attempts, unfamiliar devices, and unfamiliar locations should be reviewed when they involve personal data.

8. Incident Reporting

Suspected personal data breaches or unauthorized access must be reported through the organization's incident response process.

9. Privacy by Design

Privacy and security considerations should be incorporated into systems, applications, processes, and data handling activities.

10. Compliance

Personal data processing should comply with applicable organizational policies and data protection requirements.
