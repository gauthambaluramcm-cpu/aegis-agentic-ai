AEGIS INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for detecting, investigating, containing, and responding to security incidents.

2. Detection

Potential security incidents may be identified through security monitoring, anomaly detection, user reports, system alerts, or other sources.

3. Triage

Detected security alerts should be reviewed and prioritized based on factors such as severity, affected systems, affected data, user activity, and potential impact.

4. Investigation

Security personnel should investigate suspicious activity using relevant access logs, user information, timestamps, source locations, devices, actions, and accessed datasets.

5. Containment

Appropriate containment measures may include restricting access, disabling accounts, terminating sessions, blocking suspicious activity, or isolating affected systems.

6. Verification

After containment, security personnel should verify that suspicious activity has stopped and that affected systems and data are appropriately protected.

7. Escalation

Significant incidents should be escalated to appropriate security, privacy, legal, compliance, or management personnel according to organizational procedures.

8. Evidence Preservation

Relevant logs, alerts, system information, and other evidence should be preserved to support investigation and potential reporting requirements.

9. Recovery

Affected systems or accounts should be restored to a secure operational state following appropriate investigation and remediation.

10. Documentation

Security incidents and response activities should be documented, including the nature of the incident, evidence reviewed, actions taken, and outcome.

11. Lessons Learned

Significant incidents should be reviewed after resolution to identify improvements to security controls, monitoring, policies, and response procedures.
