AEGIS ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of organizational systems, applications, datasets, and information resources.

2. Authorized Use

Organizational systems and data must be used only for legitimate business, operational, research, or other explicitly authorized purposes.

3. Unauthorized Access

Users must not attempt to access systems, applications, datasets, or information for which they do not have authorization.

4. Excessive Data Access

Users should not access, download, export, or process data beyond what is reasonably required for their assigned responsibilities.

5. Credential Sharing

Users must not share account credentials or allow another person to use their account.

6. Security Controls

Users must not intentionally bypass, disable, or interfere with security controls, authentication mechanisms, monitoring systems, or access restrictions.

7. Data Disclosure

Organizational data must not be disclosed, transferred, or shared with unauthorized individuals or external parties.

8. Suspicious Activity

Users should promptly report suspected unauthorized access, credential compromise, data leakage, or other security incidents.

9. Monitoring

System activity may be monitored for security, compliance, operational, and incident investigation purposes.

10. Compliance

Users are expected to comply with applicable organizational policies, security requirements, and data protection obligations.
