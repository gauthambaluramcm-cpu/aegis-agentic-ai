AEGIS ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines requirements for controlling access to organizational systems, applications, datasets, and personal data. Access must be granted only to authorized users for legitimate business purposes.

2. Least Privilege

Users must receive only the minimum level of access required to perform their assigned responsibilities. Access permissions should be reviewed periodically and removed when no longer required.

3. Authorized Access

Users may access datasets and systems only when they have appropriate authorization. Access outside the user's assigned responsibilities may be treated as suspicious activity and must be investigated.

4. Bulk Data Access

Large-scale access, downloads, exports, or extraction of records should be performed only when there is a legitimate business requirement and appropriate authorization.

5. Privileged Access

Privileged accounts must be restricted to authorized personnel. Privileged activities should be monitored and reviewed.

6. Suspicious Access

Access involving unusually large data volumes, repeated failed attempts, unusual access times, unfamiliar locations, or unfamiliar devices should be reviewed by the security team.

7. Credential Protection

Users must not share passwords, authentication credentials, API keys, or other access credentials with other individuals.

8. Access Review

Access permissions should be reviewed periodically to identify excessive, unnecessary, or inappropriate privileges.

9. Investigation

When suspicious access is identified, relevant access logs, user information, timestamps, device information, location information, and accessed datasets should be preserved for investigation.

10. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in access restrictions, investigation, escalation, or other appropriate organizational action.
