AEGIS DATA RETENTION POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines principles for retaining and deleting organizational information and personal data.

2. Purpose-Based Retention

Data should be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its legitimate business, operational, legal, security, or compliance purpose.

3. Access Logs

Security and access logs should be retained for an appropriate period to support monitoring, investigation, auditing, and incident response.

4. Personal Data

Personal data should not be retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or processed, subject to applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

5. Secure Deletion

Data that is no longer required should be securely deleted or otherwise disposed of in accordance with organizational procedures.

6. Investigation Hold

Information relevant to an ongoing security investigation or incident should not be deleted until the investigation has been appropriately completed.

7. Access Control

Retained information must be protected using appropriate access controls and security safeguards.

8. Periodic Review

Retention requirements should be reviewed periodically to determine whether information continues to require retention.

9. Compliance

Retention and deletion practices should comply with applicable organizational policies and data protection requirements.
